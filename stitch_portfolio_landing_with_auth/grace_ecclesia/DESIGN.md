## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a sanctuary-like digital environment for administration, ministry leaders, and congregants. It projects reverent calm, clarity, and institutional dignity. The aesthetic departs from cluttered operational software by embracing generous breathing room, intentional composition, and quiet authority.

### Design Movement
**Minimalist Editorial Modern:** A restrained balance of warm clarity, crisp architectural borders, deep navy contrast, and sacred warm amber highlights. The aesthetic honors tradition through typography and warmth, while executing interactions with razor-sharp modern SaaS discipline.

### Emotional Response
Users should feel unburdened, focused, and welcome. The interface eliminates cognitive tension through calm negative space, intentional hierarchy, and unhurried layouts.

## Colors

The color palette establishes structural gravitas via sanctuary navy tones, offset by sacred warm amber for high-intent actions and spiritual indicators.

- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep sanctuary slate navy. Applied to prominent titles, primary buttons, active structural navigation, and high-emphasis icons.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` / hover `#B45309`)**: Sacred amber. Reserved strictly for key affirmative CTAs, active highlight tabs, donation interactions, and critical state milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#1E293B`)**: Mid-tone slate navy. Serves as supporting framing, dark borders, secondary navigation headers, and modal backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC`)**: Canvas canvas-tier background. Soft cool porcelain foundation that prevents eye fatigue while keeping content surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) luminous.
- **Border / Divider (`#E2E8F0`)**: Subtle warm slate hairline stroke, delineating layout structures without introducing visual weight.
- **Subdued Text (`#64748B`)**: Secondary text and metadata with accessible contrast ratios against white and neutral surfaces.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans provides geometric warmth, high x-height readability, and a friendly yet structured presence.

- **Editorial Headings**: Display and primary headlines utilize negative letter spacing to project craft and precision.
- **Airy Body Text**: Paragraphs mandate a line-height multiplier between 1.6x and 1.7x to safeguard against dense data compression and visual exhaustion.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Upper-cased or small labels receive slight tracking (`0.01em` to `0.03em`) for legibility in tables, role badges, and form controls.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an uncrowded 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to a maximum content width of 1440px for web applications, centering the user's focus.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `mobile`: `< 768px` — 4-column layout, single-column authentication cards, stacked navigation.
  - `tablet`: `768px - 1024px` — 8-column layout, collateral sidebars collapse into slide-out sheets.
  - `desktop`: `> 1024px` — 12-column layout, permanent left sanctuary rail (280px), generous main workspace.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Spacing tokens are intentionally generous. High-level dashboard cards and forms prefer `space-lg` (2rem) and `space-xl` (3.5rem) to separate distinct functional panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp, light architectural boundaries and delicate ambient light rather than heavy drop shadows.

- **Surface Layers**: Canvas sits on `#F8FAFC`. Base components (cards, auth containers, table cells) sit atop pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines**: Surface panels use a strict 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Ambient Light Shadows**:
  - *Resting Card*: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
  - *Elevated (Dropdown, Modal, Hovered Panel)*: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Modals & Overlays**: Backdrop scrim is tinted with sanctuary navy `#0F172A` at 35% opacity, paired with an 8px backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-sm`).

## Shapes

The system uses standard roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` base) to balance architectural stability with human approachability.

- **Buttons & Form Inputs**: `rounded-md` (`0.5rem`) for crisp, grounded touch targets.
- **Cards & Auth Panels**: `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) paired with 1px slate borders.
- **Modals & Flyouts**: `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`) providing a gentle container silhouette.
- **Status Badges & Avatar Containers**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) for quick organic recognition.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#1E293B`, active `#020617`. Height 44px (md), horizontal padding 20px. Font weight 600.
- **Sacred / Highlight**: Background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#B45309`. Used for primary giving, member baptism check-ins, or major milestone actions.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: White background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, text `#0F172A`, hover background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Focus State**: 2px offset ring in `#D97706`.

### Input Fields & Authentication Controls
- **Resting**: Height 44px, white background, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- **Focus**: Border `#0F172A` with a soft 3px box-shadow halo in `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Error**: Border `#DC2626`, halo `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.1)`, with validation microcopy in 0.75rem red text accompanied by an icon.
- **Floating Labels / Fieldsets**: Generous label clearance with 6px vertical spacing above inputs.

### Role & Status Badges
- **Elder / Pastor**: Amber tint (`#FEF3C7` background, `#92400E` text, `#FDE68A` border).
- **Ministry Leader**: Navy tint (`#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` text, `#CBD5E1` border).
- **Member / Congregant**: Sage green tint (`#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, `#A7F3D0` border).
- **Format**: Pill-shaped, font size 0.75rem, font weight 600, padding 4px 10px.

### Cards & Sanctuary Panels
- **Structure**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, padding 24px (tablet/mobile: 18px), gentle elevation blur.
- **Auth Card Variant**: Max width 440px, centered layout, 40px internal padding, subtle gold top border hairline (`2px solid #D97706`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes**: 18px x 18px, rounded 4px, unchecked border 1.5px `#CBD5E1`, checked background `#0F172A` with crisp white check mark icon.
- **Radio Buttons**: 18px x 18px circular frame, checked inner dot `#0F172A` with 4px margin.

### Member & Ministry Lists
- Rows have minimum height of 64px, internal cell padding `16px 20px`, separated by 1px `#F1F5F9` dividers.
- Hover state transitions subtly to `#F8FAFC` background without jarring movement.
